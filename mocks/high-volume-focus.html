Checked on 7 October

What the Tax team’s docs already decided

We read the docs in the Tax app folder of the Dayshape repo. A few things this board treated as open were settled weeks ago. Here they are.

  • Phase order is a set of links. Each phase can come after one or more phases. No links means no order. Booking dates never invent one. Agreed on 15 September in ADR 0022.
  • The links live on the engagement. You set them in a Configure window on the Details tab, and they save with the engagement. They store no overlap rule. They never move a booking.
  • Ready comes from two things. A yes or no field on the engagement called Work released opens the first phases. A later phase is ready when every phase before it is done. The pill is built and only warns.
  • Phase Complete is built. It deletes your own later bookings on that phase and nobody else’s. You cannot undo it.
  • Pooled work in 26.6 is gated by the Pool Worker trait. Take one pooled booking and you take every open booking for that phase and requirement, in every week.

Settled enough to keep building

  • A card is a booking. The board is a week.
  • Start, complete, defer to next week, and on hold are the moves.
  • Defer moves this week’s booking forward exactly one week. It asks nothing and records nothing. It only warns if the new week breaks the deadline.
  • On hold is different. It asks why, when the work unblocks, and how much is done. The rest goes into a new booking in the unblock week.
  • A different actual effort updates that booking. Capacity for this week is the point of doing it immediately.
  • Phase Complete deletes your own later bookings on that phase. Other people’s stay. If you have no later bookings, nobody asks.
  • Phase order is a set of links on the engagement. A phase can come after one or more phases. No links means no order.
  • Overlap between phases is allowed. The links do not stop it. Overtake should be a signal, not a block, so customers can tell us if they want the stop.
  • Readiness is not the column. Work released, a yes or no field on the engagement, opens the first phases. A later phase is ready when every phase before it is done. The weak spot: review says Not ready while it overlaps prep.
  • Not every practitioner sees every unassigned booking. For 26.6 only people with the Pool Worker trait do. It is a stopgap, and Boney does not want it to become the real model. The docs say the same.

Still open

  • Workflow as the thing that moves Kanban status. The boolean is the fallback. Workflow can sit on top later. Calum still needs a session, with real use cases, before that is designed.
  • What a pool is. US pool work is different from UK work that mixes pool and advisory. Prep can be pooled when sign off is not. “The pool” is the wrong noun.
  • Automatic allocation, a utilisation buffer under a full week, and advise filling people to the ceiling.
  • A practitioner move that also moves a colleague’s diary.
  • Kanban moves that raise suggestions. For this work the room wants practitioners to make the change. Other service lines may want the opposite.
  • A stored completion date on the phase. Derive it from the last booking.
  • One Gantt row per booking, or a new hours setting.
  • Where phase dates live. Phase order already sits on the Details tab. For now the dates can live in Timeframe. The Gantt is where people want to see them.

Two different moves

Defer is not on hold

Defer is the quick kick to next week. Customers asked for it as its own action. It moves this week’s booking forward exactly seven days. It asks nothing and records nothing. If the new week breaks the deadline, it warns you first. Later bookings stay where they are.

On hold means the work is blocked. It asks why, when you expect it to unblock, and how much you already did. The rest goes into a new booking in the unblock week. The window already shows how late each later phase could be, with a red badge. It does not say who that delay lands on. In the session demo the split also failed once on a booking that was already in the future.

Nothing moved. On hold starts from the 5 hours already on the booking.
Not agreed

What happens to the next phase

Today nothing moves. Phase links never move a booking, and the on hold window only shows the delay. If review is still unassigned, Steven expects it to follow prep, so it is not left in a week nobody will pick up. In August the team leaned the same way: only move follow on work that nobody has yet.

If Happy is already booked to review, moving her week because Jose slipped feels intrusive. Leaving her in the past is wrong too. Boney said it depends, and a portfolio could be set anywhere from “do not move” to “move and reassign”. This prototype shows the flag and the move. It does not pretend one of them won.

Pooled work is not “everything unassigned”

The current build shows every unassigned booking to anyone with the Pool Worker trait. That stops partners grabbing the shelf, which is what 26.6 needs. It is not a definition of a pool.

Boney split it into two questions. Am I allowed to take pooled work at all? Which pooled work do I see? The docs split it the same way. The trait says who may take work. Pool eligibility, set on the phase later, says which work. Firms already have several pools, often by phase. A list of 600 cards is not a queue. The second sketch is something to show at the next customer drop in, not a design to build.
