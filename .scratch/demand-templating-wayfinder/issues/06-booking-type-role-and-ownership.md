## Question

What role should booking types play in demand templates?

The latest discussion established that:

- Booking types are optional in a non-phase world.
- Booking types may also be optional tags in a phase world.
- Multiple booking types may be attached to work.
- A primary booking type may drive colour in the existing product.

The remaining decision is:

- Are booking types required in the template at all, or are they added later to tidy or classify demand?
- What does a booking type mean: work nature, return type, industry, service line, or something else?
- If multiple booking types are allowed, how is the primary one selected?
- Does phase colour override booking-type colour?
- Which customer or service-line evidence is needed before making this part of the MVP UI?
